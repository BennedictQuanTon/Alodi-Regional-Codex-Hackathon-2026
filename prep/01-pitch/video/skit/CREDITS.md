# Skit media

All characters and the heart are drawn in `compositions/skit.html`.

| File | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `assets/audio/press.wav` | HyperFrames bundled sounds, from Pixabay | Pixabay Content License |
| `assets/audio/t*.wav` | Kokoro-82M voices, from `script.json` via `../tts.py` | Apache-2.0 |
| `assets/audio/music.wav` and other effects | Generated by `../audio.py` | Team |

Emoji come from Windows' Segoe UI Emoji font, so render on Windows. The final volume step runs after rendering:

`ffmpeg -i renders/skit.mp4 -c:v copy -af "volume=4dB,alimiter=limit=0.8:level=disabled" -c:a aac -b:a 256k -movflags +faststart ../alodi-skit.mp4`
